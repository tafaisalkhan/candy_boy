# Privacy Policy for Boy Favorite Collection

**Last Updated:** November 12, 2026

**Boy Favorite Collection** ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Supreme Traders, respects the privacy of our players ("you", "user"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play our mobile game.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect (No Personal Data)
We do **not** collect, store, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), such as:
* Your real name, physical address, or phone number
* Your email address or contact lists
* Your biometric data, location coordinates, or device camera/microphone data
* Any government-issued identification

---

## 2. Local Device Storage (Game Progress & Save Data)
The Game saves gameplay data strictly on your local device using encrypted and sandboxed configuration files. This data includes:
* Current level progression and unlocked levels
* In-game coins and collected items
* Audio preferences (music and sound effects volume)
* Privacy policy acceptance confirmation

This information remains entirely on your device and is never uploaded to external servers.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)
The Game uses **Google AdMob** to display non-intrusive advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads) to support free gameplay.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device identifiers and diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:
* Device advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / IDFA)
* Device model, operating system version, and general network connection type
* Coarse location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
* Ad interaction events (such as ad impressions, clicks, or video completion)

You can review Google’s privacy practices and learn how Google uses data from partner apps here:
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [Google Advertising Technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

You can opt-out of personalized advertisements at any time through your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID**).

---

## 4. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance
We are dedicated to safeguarding children's privacy. **Boy Favorite Collection** is designed for a general audience of all ages:
* We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction).
* Ads served are configured to be child-safe and family-friendly.
* If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has provided us with personal information without consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt action.

---

## 5. Data Security
We implement standard industry security practices to safeguard all local game files. Because we do not operate remote databases storing your personal information, your exposure to remote data breaches is minimized.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or game features. Any updates will be posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date.
